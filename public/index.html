How to Play Sudoku

Sudoku is played on a 9×9 grid divided into nine 3×3 sub-grids. The goal is to fill all empty cells with numbers from 1 to 9 according to three simple rules:

  • Row Constraint: Each horizontal row must contain every number from 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Column Constraint: Each vertical column must contain every number from 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Box Constraint: Each 3×3 box must contain every number from 1 to 9 exactly once.

No guessing is necessary for properly formed puzzles. Use logic, eliminate impossible candidates, and use our Intelligent Hint Engine whenever you want to learn new solving strategies!
